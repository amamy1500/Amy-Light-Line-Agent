  and sends you each new lead. It can put a caller through to you.
- **It only says what you approve.** It never gives prices, dates, discounts or promises. If it doesn't
  know, it says you'll cover it when you call back.

## What it does for you

- Answers every call, day or night, so no customer gets voicemail and gives up.
- Asks what you'd ask: what they'd like lit, the address, home size, outdoor plugs, timing, name,
  email, and the best time to call back.
- Puts the caller through to your cell when they ask for you (you press 1 to take it). If you can't
  pick up, the caller goes back to the assistant and nothing is lost.
- Sends you each lead by email now, and by text once texting is approved.
- Screens spam: blocked numbers never ring, robocalls are cut short, and likely spam is counted in one
  morning summary instead of a text each.
- Spots emergencies. If someone mentions sparks, smoke or an injury, it tells them to call 911 first,
  then texts you as urgent.
- Gives you a short "before you call" note on each lead: who they are, what they want, and what they
  asked that only you can answer.

## Questions you might have

**Will callers know it's not a person?** Yes. It says so at the start and whenever asked. It never
pretends to be you.

**What if it says something wrong?** It only uses words you approved. Every call is checked for prices,
dates and promises, and anything odd is flagged for you to look at.

**How do I teach it answers?** On the "Answers to approve" page. Each common question has a box for
your own words. Until you fill it in, the assistant says you'll cover it on the callback.

**What if a caller insists on talking to me?** It offers to put them through. If you're busy, it takes
the details and sets a callback, so they don't repeat themselves.

**What about all the spam?** Numbers you block never ring. Hidden numbers must press 1 first. Likely
spam doesn't text you; it shows up in one morning summary so you can check it.

**What if something breaks?** Callers can always leave a voicemail. No call is dropped.

**Can I turn it off?** Yes, one switch in the console. Your phone rings during your hours and callers
leave messages otherwise.

**Will it book appointments?** No. You said callbacks only, and that's how it works.

**Who sees the calls?** Only people with the console login. Calls are recorded with a notice at the
start; callers can say no. Card numbers are removed automatically.

**Why do you need our EIN?** Phone carriers require business registration before an app can text, and
to lift the limit of 2 calls at once. It's the same information on your IRS letter.

**Why does texting take about two weeks?** The carriers review every business that sends texts from an
app. Until then, alerts come by email.

**What happens in January with the new hire?** One change in Settings: their name and cell. The
assistant starts using their name right away.

**Can it speak Spanish?** It can, once a Spanish speaker checks the greeting. It's off until then.
